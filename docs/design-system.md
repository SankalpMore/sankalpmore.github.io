# Dockio design and content system

## Direction

An approachable, AI-value-focused systems integrator: business priorities connect to governed delivery, everyday adoption, and evidence of value. The open-dock mark is an original open D enclosure receiving a small rectangular module: connection, clear boundaries, and a place for work to move forward.

The October 2026 direction uses warm ivory (#F5F2EA), deep ink (#172B32), vermilion (#EE603C), and sage (#DCE5D6). Large editorial sans-serif headlines, comfortable body text, open numbered service columns, a connected value path, a human workshop photo, and a purposeful BudgetOps evidence cycle provide the visual language. No synthetic dashboards or client-logo walls.

## Implementation contract

- Max-width 1280px; desktop gutters 48–64px, mobile 22px. Hero heading 56–92px; section titles 40–64px; body 17–20px. System sans typography avoids external font requests.
- Header: dockio mark; Solutions, Industries, How we work, BudgetOps, About; Share your requirement. Mobile menu expands below header with matching links.
- Hero: AI transformation & systems integration; Big ambition. Real-world AI.; supplied business-priorities-to-adoption copy; Share your requirement; See how we work.
- Photo is a generated illustrative collaboration scene, never presented as Dockio staff or clients. No founder portrait unless provided by the founder.
- Service columns: Choose what matters; Make the work happen; Help the change stick. Sage working-model section. Dark BudgetOps section with an accessible vector evidence cycle. Direct enquiry section.
- The BudgetOps section is a vision for better investment decisions, not a released product, pricing offer, or client result.
- Arrows, logo, and explanatory diagram are scalable vector artwork; body copy and all controls remain HTML. Focus states, keyboard behavior, and reduced-motion support are required.
- Contact creates a local reviewable email brief. No backend, analytics, persistence, or automatic sending.

## Search and source

Pre-render each route to substantive static HTML with its own title, description, canonical URL, and structured data matching visible content. Keep legacy hash links working. Use canonical www.dockio.in URLs across the Sites preview. Keep sitemap and internal links in the build from the same route inventory.

GitHub is canonical. Update the same draft PR and Sites project; do not merge or change DNS during review. See README for release parity.

## Solution and industry depth

The Solutions and Industries pages are first-class navigation destinations. Existing URL paths remain canonical. Every service, industry and use-case detail page has a specific operating situation, an explicitly illustrative workflow, scoped Dockio deliverables, integration/review/adoption considerations, prospective measures and related work. Measures describe what to baseline and review; they never imply achieved Dockio results. Keep sector details distinct rather than substituting an industry name in generic prose.

The expanded visual system uses split image-led introductions, open situation rows, a finance-led industry feature, contextual industry thumbnails, sage workflow sequences, open deliverable lists and an ink value-review band. Same-page navigation uses normal anchors and remains usable without JavaScript. On narrow screens these stack in reading order; photographs retain their native 3:2 composition so participating people are not cropped out.

Seven generated editorial work scenes cover cross-functional collaboration, finance review, technology implementation, logistics, manufacturing quality, non-clinical healthcare operations and property review. People vary in age, gender and background and participate in the depicted work. These are illustrative fictional scenes, never staff portraits, clients or evidence of engagements. All new detail and hub photos carry an illustrative caption and descriptive alt text; thumbnails within already-labelled links use empty alt text. Source images are optimized into local 1536px and 640px WebP variants with explicit dimensions and responsive loading. Preserve useful older assets without routing visitors to obsolete visuals.

BudgetOps remains a vision. The value-review sections connect observed delivery and adoption evidence to the next continue/change/scale decision; they do not advertise a finished product.
