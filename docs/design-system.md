# Dockio design system

The October 2026 redesign presents Dockio as an enterprise AI transformation and systems-integration company. The visual direction is white editorial space, charcoal type, restrained cobalt, candid illustrative workplace photography and open service rows. It should feel human and credible to an enterprise sponsor, technology leader or procurement team.

## Tokens and layout

- White #FFFFFF, ink #151A24, cobalt #2849E8, pale periwinkle #EFF2FF, charcoal #181F2B.
- System Arial/Helvetica typography; large, tightly tracked headlines and readable 16px body copy.
- Maximum 1320px content width and responsive 24–72px gutters.
- Direct navigation links, square buttons, fine dividers and a clear mobile menu.
- Open numbered service lists, editorial industry layouts, one dark BudgetOps band and purposeful people imagery.
- SVG arrows use consistent 1.6px strokes. The existing Dockio symbol retains its geometry with the cobalt colour.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable transitions and smooth scrolling.

## Content and conversion

Homepage: ambition → services → industries → BudgetOps → human collaboration → buyer routes → substantial perspectives → FAQ → next step.

Primary action: Book a conversation. Supporting paths: initiative, RFI/RFP, embedded support and BudgetOps interest. Service and industry CTAs preserve their context in the enquiry draft. Forms prepare email briefs; they do not imply online receipt.

BudgetOps is in development. Select partner status is user-confirmed. Do not claim a higher partner tier, rankings, guaranteed ROI, an established staffing bench or previous-employer engagements as Dockio customers. Human workplace assets are generated illustrations, never labelled as actual staff or clients.

## Verification limits

The managed environment does not expose the supported control-browser capability. Per the Sites managed-preview workflow, no alternate browser path is started. Production compilation, static rendering, metadata, links, images, structured data and programmatic checks are verified separately; desktop/mobile visual fidelity and live calendar availability remain unverified.
